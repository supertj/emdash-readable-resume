Write your resume in Markdown on the **Resume** page in your admin and click Save. The plugin keeps one page in your Pages collection up to date with it: your name as the title, your contact line, then sections with a line for each job or school, the role and dates under it, and bullets.

The structure is the Classic layout from [ReadableResume](https://readableresume.com). The page is one column of headings, paragraphs and bullets in reading order, with no images or tables, so the text copies cleanly.

The page uses your theme's fonts and styles. The plugin only touches the one page it creates, makes no network requests, and never publishes on its own: you publish the page from the editor, and later saves wait as a draft until you publish again.
